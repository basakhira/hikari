FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything students usually ask before their first visit.

Do I need any Japanese before joining? +
No. Most students start from zero. Returning learners are placed at the right level so they aren't repeating basics.
How long until I'm ready for SSW or JLPT? +
It depends on your starting level and attendance. Beginners typically need 4–6 months for N5–N4; SSW candidates combine language study with skill-test prep over a similar window.
Can Hikari help with visa documents directly? +
Yes. We guide COE applications, financial documentation, and embassy submission for both study and SSW pathways.
Can I visit the center before enrolling? +
Absolutely. Walk-ins are welcome during office hours — come see a class and meet the instructors before you commit.
